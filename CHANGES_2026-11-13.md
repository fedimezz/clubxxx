# 2026-11-13 — Vercel single-address mode (`/c/<club>`)

Problem: clubs were routed by subdomain (`club.domain`). A free `*.vercel.app` address cannot do that, and
`clubxx-lovat.vercel.app` was even read as a tenant called "clubxx-lovat".

## Behaviour
- `APP_URL` on `*.vercel.app` (or `NEXT_PUBLIC_SINGLE_HOST_MODE=true`) turns on single-address mode. Real domain = unchanged.
- `/c/<slug>` opens a club (cookie `club_slug`), `/c` leaves it. `/c/<slug>/api/...` is that club's API (mobile base URL).
- `/platform`, `/onboarding` ignore the cookie. Auth cookies are host-only. `/api/settings/public` and the
  unversioned `/api/hero-model` are not CDN-cached in this mode (all clubs share one URL).
